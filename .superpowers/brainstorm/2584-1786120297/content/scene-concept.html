What should the 3D scene be?

This is the site's soul — the hero renders it full-screen, and it can persist behind sections as you scroll. Each option reacts to cursor and scroll. Click your favorite.

A

Living distributed system — 3D node graph

nodes = services · pulses = events · cursor bends the graph

A floating network of glowing nodes with event-pulses traveling the edges — literally what you build for a living. Cursor attracts/repels nodes; scrolling into a case study reconfigures the graph into that system's architecture. The 3D scene and the C-style case studies become one continuous machine.

B

Morphing particle field

SATVIK

Tens of thousands of particles that assemble into your name, then scatter and reform into shapes as you scroll (a globe, a graph, section titles). Cursor repels particles like a force field. Very cinematic, very "wow reel" — the metaphor is looser than A, it's beauty for beauty's sake.

C

Planet-scale — wireframe globe with live arcs

6 production regions · arcs = cross-region event streams

A rotating wireframe Earth with your 6 production regions marked, event-stream arcs firing between them (the GitHub-homepage globe, but it's your infrastructure). Drag to spin, hover a region for stats. Strong "I operate at global scale" message; slightly more familiar/less novel than A.

D

Abstract shader — aurora / fluid field

GPU fluid reacts to cursor like disturbing water

A full-screen GLSL shader — flowing aurora/ink that warps around the cursor and shifts hue per section. Most elegant and least literal; the site feels like art. Lightest to run of the four, but says "designer" more than "systems engineer".

My recommendation: A (node graph). Maximum wow and maximum argument — the showpiece is a simulation of the thing you're hired to build, and it merges naturally with the scrollytelling case studies you also liked. B is the runner-up if you want pure spectacle.
