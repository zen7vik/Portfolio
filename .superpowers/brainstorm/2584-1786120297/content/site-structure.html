How should the site be structured?

The particle scene is the constant; this decides where the depth lives. Click one.

A

One epic page — the journey

PARTICLES form "SATVIK" · tagline · scroll cue
About strip — particles reform into a globe behind stats particles morph
Case study 1: Risk engine — pinned scrollytelling diagram particles morph
Case study 2 · Case study 3 (same treatment)
Projects + Writing cards
Contact — particles swirl into an @ sign · resume download

Everything on one continuous scroll; the particle field morphs at every section boundary. Most cinematic — one unbroken performance. Cons: a long, heavy page (WebGL + several pinned sequences); case studies can't be linked individually; depth is capped or the page becomes exhausting.

B

Showpiece landing + deep-dive case pages

LANDING: particles + about + 3 case study CARDS + contact
↓ click a card — particles dissolve into the next page
/work/risk-engine
full scrollytelling
/work/data-exchange
full scrollytelling
/work/rag-pipeline
full scrollytelling

Landing stays fast and jaw-dropping; each case study is its own page with room for real depth (animated architecture, metrics, "what I'd do differently"). Individually shareable links for recruiters; page transitions become another wow moment. Cons: more pages to build; some visitors never click through.

C

Hybrid — full story on one page, expandable depth

One page like A, but each case study is a teaser…
Case study teaser → "expand" opens an immersive overlay with the full deep-dive overlay

Single-page flow with case studies expanding in-place (full-screen overlay). Middle ground on effort and depth. Cons: overlays are fiddly on mobile, deep-links are hackier, and it's the least conventional to navigate.

My recommendation: B. A recruiter gets the wow + full story in 60 seconds on the landing page; a hiring manager gets shareable, deep case studies. It also keeps the heavy scrollytelling off the landing page, so the particle scene stays at 60fps.
