Case studies are the star: as you scroll, live architecture diagrams pin and animate — events flowing through your pipeline, latency dropping when the story hits your reliability push. particles react to your cursor

Three.js hero scene — a particle field / node graph that reacts to the cursor, sections emerging from the 3D world. Maximum wow. Risks: heaviest to build and tune, perf on laptops, and for a backend job search it can read style-over-substance unless executed perfectly.

My recommendation: A as the base + C's animated-diagram case studies, with B's ⌘K palette as a power-user touch. That combination is distinctive, deeply interactive, and every bit of motion argues you're a good engineer rather than decorating. But pick what excites you — you'll be the one sharing this link.
